## Final prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero photograph for Bonitas Foundation, a Christian foundation whose visual identity centres on faith, renewal, patient growth and hope. Generate a single beautiful photorealistic editorial landscape photograph, no graphic design. A tranquil field of ripe wheat in southern Scandinavia at the very end of a summer day. Close natural wheat stems in the lower foreground, a softly rolling field, a distant line of olive-green trees, and a tiny understated historic Scandinavian church spire far in the background, partly softened by atmospheric haze. Low warm sunlight enters from the upper right, subtle golden backlighting and authentic natural texture, restrained colour grade with deep olive greens and honey gold, muted pale sky. Serene, tactile, hopeful, credible rather than fantastical; sophisticated medium-format landscape photography, selective depth of field without excessive blur. Wide landscape composition, 3:2 aspect ratio, image intended to crop well into a tall right-hand hero panel as well as a wide banner; place a few sharply defined wheat heads around the centre and keep the distant spire near the right-hand third. No people, no words, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no frames, no collages, no artificial lens flare or exaggerated orange saturation.
